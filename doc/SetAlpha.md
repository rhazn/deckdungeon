# Scenario Cards

## Combat
Negative combat scenarios that introduce enemies to the dungeon. Examples: "Spawn X-Y enemies of type Z".

## Loot
Positive loot scenarios that grant resources or items to the party. Examples: "Earn X items of type Y".

## Effect
Scenarios that apply temporary buffs or debuffs to the party:
- **Positive Effect**: Swipe to deal X% more damage for the next Y cards, or swipe to heal the party for X%.
- **Negative Effect**: Deal X% damage to the party, or lower party damage by Y%. May include conditions based on party composition (e.g., "Take 3 damage if you don't have an elf in your party") or character stats (e.g., "Deal 3 damage if no character has initiative > 3").

## Deck Interaction
Scenarios that modify the dungeon deck itself:
- Look at the next card and choose to put it at the bottom.
- Shuffle the deck.
- Shuffle X cards of type Y into the deck.

## Choice
Scenarios that offer a selection of cards to add to a character's deck, either temporarily (cards disappear after the dungeon) or permanently (cards remain after the dungeon).

## Random
Scenarios that present random outcomes or card reveals, adding unpredictability to the dungeon run.

## Boss
Boss scenario cards that serve as the final encounter of the dungeon. The boss card is inserted into the bottom 30% of the dungeon deck and ends the dungeon when revealed and handled.
